# Voice, Video & Media Features

## Overview
Covers:
- **Voice Notes Studio**: Hold-to-record gesture, slide-to-cancel, lock-to-handsfree, live waveform amplitude visualizer, pre-send preview, and 1x/1.5x/2x in-chat audio player.
- **Circular Video Notes**: Telegram-style circular quick video messages.
- **In-App Camera & Media Editor**: In-app viewfinder, crop, doodle, sticker overlays, and client-side media compression.
- **Sticker & GIF Ecosystem**: Telegram `.tgs` animated vector stickers, in-app AI sticker maker with background cutout, and Giphy search.
